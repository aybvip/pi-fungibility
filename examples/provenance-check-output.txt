ℹ️  Note on M addresses (Muxed Accounts, CAP-27):
      • M = a G address + a 64-bit id; the G account is what exists on the ledger.
      • The network treats it exactly as if no muxing were used (same native pi).
      • If from_muxed/to_muxed appear, they identify the counterparty only,
        not the coin's origin. Differentiation remains impossible.
